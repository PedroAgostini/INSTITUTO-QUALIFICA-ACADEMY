Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how Qualifica Academy may collect, use, and protect information related to visitors, leads, subscribers, and students.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as name, email, payment-related data, course access information, form submissions, and communication preferences.

2. Automatic Data

We may use cookies, analytics, Meta Pixel, and similar technologies to understand page visits, campaign performance, device data, browser information, and interactions with the landing page.

3. How We Use Data

Data may be used to provide access, process subscriptions, improve content, support students, send relevant communications, measure campaigns, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We may share limited data with service providers such as payment platforms, learning platforms, email tools, analytics providers, advertising platforms, and technical hosting services when necessary to operate the experience.

5. Your Choices

You may request updates, corrections, or deletion of personal information when applicable. You may also manage cookies and advertising preferences through your browser or platform settings.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no online system is completely risk-free.

7. Contact

For privacy requests, contact Instituto Qualifica through the official communication channels provided during enrollment.
